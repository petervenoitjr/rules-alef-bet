# Alef-Bet Rules — handoff (v0.2.0, 2026-11-10)

A Rules! (TheCodingMonkeys, 2014) style memory mode for the Alef-Bet app. DO-T gives one new rule per level; rules stack, newest first, rule 1 always clears the board by value. 20 levels in two batches of 10; the stack resets between batches.

## v0.2.0: a Hebrew teaching game
- **40 levels, 4 stacks:** NUMBERS, ALEF-BET ORDER, SOUNDS (letter names hidden on the tiles; rules by sound, silent letters, two-sound letters), PSALM 119 (DO-T quotes the first verse of a letter's part; find the letter that begins it in Hebrew).
- **Letter Book:** all 22 letters, laid out right to left. A letter turns green the first time you tap it right. Each card shows the sound (modern Hebrew), number value, final form, its Psalm 119 part (KJV heading, verses, "all 8 verses begin with" check), the first Hebrew word of the part (WLC, vowel points, accents left out) with its Strong's number, and the KJV first verse.
- **DID YOU KNOW:** 14 facts on the rule card. Facts about Scripture show only when `extract.py` verified them (Psalm 119 stanzas, Proverbs 31:10-31, Lamentations 1-4, Psalm 145 has no NUN verse).
- **Name meanings are empty on purpose:** `LETTER_NAME_MEANINGS` in SETTINGS waits for Peter's Alef-Bet book (rule 6 of the Scripture Line rules).

## Scripture sources and audit (same method as The Shepherd's Beat)
- `src-data/extract.py` pulls everything by program: WLC from openscriptures/morphhb, KJV from 4 copies (aruljohn, thiagobodruk, scrollmapper, open-bibles OSIS), majority reading. Output: `letter-data.js` (generated, never hand-edited).
- `src-data/audit.py` blocks delivery: every "quote" (Psalm c:v, KJV) in the built sketch must be in that verse; every Psalm 119 entry re-checked; every verse rule must point at the first verse of its part. Last run: 32 PASS, 0 FAIL.
- **Open question:** the two KJV copies that print the Psalm 119 headings differ at verse 137: TZADDE (open-bibles OSIS) vs TZADDI (scrollmapper). The card shows both. Pick one against your printed KJV.

## Kit bug found (fix in the master skill)
`ahad-feel.js` `drawGreatJob()` calls `textStyle(BOLD)`. In p5 2.3.4, `textStyle(BOLD)` after any `textWeight()` call (DOT-UI's `txt` uses textWeight) freezes `textSize`, so GREAT JOB! drew at 9 px. Fix used here: `try { textWeight(800); } catch (_) { textStyle(BOLD); }`. The master copy in `~/.claude/skills/ahad-feel/assets/ahad-feel.js` still has the bug.

## Design decisions
- Modes: TIMELESS (default for children: no clock, HINT button, DO-T shows the rule after 2 misses on one rule, amber brackets on the right tiles) and CHALLENGE (20 s clock, +0.5 s per correct tap, −1 s per miss, max 30 s).
- Rule design rule (in SETTINGS): a newer rule must never contain every letter of an older one, or the older rule never gets a turn. Batch 1 was reordered after the first test found "the ones" swallowing "ALEF" and "the tens" swallowing all five final-form letters.
- Board maker keeps the fairest of up to 400 random boards, then recolors tiles to repair near-misses. Levels 10 and 20 still leave one rule empty on about 4–10% of boards; the game skips an empty rule, as Rules! does.
- Letter values are standard (Alef 1 … Tav 400), used for counting only; no gematria meanings.
